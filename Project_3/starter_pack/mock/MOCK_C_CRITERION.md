# Mock criterion C: does the reply start with the answer?

Decide whether the reply **starts with the answer** to the customer's question.

- `pass`: the first sentence already answers the question or gives information about it.
- `fail`: the reply opens with a sentence that is only a greeting, thanks, compliment or other pleasantry
  (e.g. "Thank you for your message.", "Good question!", "Happy to help.") before the answer.

A short phrase inside an answering sentence ("No problem: there is a grace period ...") is fine. Pleasantries later
in the reply do not matter. Whether the content is correct or matches the policy does not matter for this criterion.
Language and register do not matter.
