# Judge 2 criterion: does the reply state the policy correctly?

Decide whether every policy statement in the reply is **correct** according to the policy: numbers, conditions,
scope and details. Errors count in both directions (more generous or less generous than the policy). Leaving out a
condition the policy attaches to a statement, or widening its scope, makes the statement incorrect.

- `pass`: every policy statement in the reply is correct (a reply that states no policy fact passes).
- `fail`: at least one statement misstates the policy.

Tone, length, politeness, register and language do not matter.
