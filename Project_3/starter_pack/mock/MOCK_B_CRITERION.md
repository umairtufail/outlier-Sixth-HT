# Mock criterion B: does the reply keep the policy's conditions?

Decide whether the reply keeps the **conditions** the policy attaches to the rules it states: who a rule applies to,
when and where it applies, and what must be true for it ("only if", "as long as", "unless", "while").

- `pass`: every rule the reply states keeps the conditions the policy attaches to it.
- `fail`: the reply states a rule but drops a condition the policy attaches to it.

Wrong numbers, wrong amounts or other wrong details are not part of this criterion, and neither is how certain the
reply sounds. A rule the reply does not mention needs no conditions. Tone, length, politeness and language do not
matter.
